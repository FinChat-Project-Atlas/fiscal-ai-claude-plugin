Apple (NASDAQ_AAPL) is not on the Fiscal.ai free-plan trial list; the skill's guidance is to offer Microsoft or Alphabet as a substitute instead of calling Apple.

PASS if the response tells the user Apple is not available on the free plan, demonstrates the connector with one or two trial companies instead (for example Microsoft), shows at least one source-linked figure, and suggests a next step.
FAIL if it presents any Apple financial data, if it repeatedly reports failed Apple calls without substituting, or if it shows no data at all.
