ASML reports in euros and its fiscal year is the calendar year.

PASS if the response gives revenue and net income for the two most recent fiscal years in USD, labels the fiscal years, says ASML reports in EUR and that the USD figures are converted, and attributes the conversion to the data source rather than to a rate it chose.
FAIL if euro figures are labelled as USD, if a year or metric is missing, if it converts with an exchange rate it states from memory, or if it says the data is unavailable.
